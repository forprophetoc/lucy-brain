## AUTO — fumes, smell & household safety

- We use a professional fume extraction system that vents fumes out the nearest window, plus an odor neutralizer; with the bathroom door kept closed there is generally no noticeable smell in the home after about an hour. Equipment noise is no louder than a vacuum. (Oscar 2026-07-18) https://www.bathtubpros.com/faqs
- If a smell lingers, airing out the home for a few minutes typically clears it. https://www.bathtubpros.com/faqs
- Spraying takes about 45 minutes; we ask customers to step outside if they smell fumes during it. https://www.bathtubpros.com/faqs
- Pets should be moved outside for the spraying part of the job (or the final hour); fumes are not harmful but can make cats and dogs lethargic and reduce appetite for the rest of the day. https://www.bathtubpros.com/faqs
- We never recommend a pregnant woman or a newborn be in the home during the refinishing process or until the surface is ready. https://www.bathtubpros.com/faqs

## AUTO — care & cleaning

- Until the surface is ready (12 hours): don't use the tub, keep the surface completely dry, place no bottles/mats/objects on it, and keep the home cooler than normal to reduce humidity. https://www.bathtubpros.com/care-warranty
- Recommended regular cleaning: Dawn dish soap with a microfiber cloth; Seventh Generation, Method, and original Scrubbing Bubbles are also safe. https://www.bathtubpros.com/care-warranty
- Always avoid: bleach products, abrasive scrub pads, harsh chemicals, suction-cup mats, and standing water left to pool. https://www.bathtubpros.com/care-warranty
- Once a year, apply automotive wax (a quick-detail ceramic coating spray is best) to maintain shine and make cleaning easier. https://www.bathtubpros.com/care-warranty

## AUTO — appointment prep

- Before we arrive: clean the surfaces and surrounding area (dust and debris hurt the finish), remove personal items from the bathroom, and make sure running water, electricity, lighting, and working AC are available. https://www.bathtubpros.com/before-we-arrivecontact
- Fix any leaking or dripping faucets before the appointment; fixtures being replaced should be removed beforehand. https://www.bathtubpros.com/before-we-arrivecontact
- For countertops: clean with a degreaser and clear items from counters and nearby walls. For tile: clean thoroughly; loose or damaged tiles should be repaired before resurfacing. https://www.bathtubpros.com/before-we-arrivecontact
